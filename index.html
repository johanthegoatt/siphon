Siphon

Find every subscription hiding in your bank statement.

Export a CSV from your bank or card, drop it here, and get a list of every recurring charge: what it costs a year, which prices went up, which ones stopped, and when each renews next.

How do I get a CSV from my bank?
  1. Sign in to your bank or card website on a computer.
  2. Open the account, then look for Download, Export or Statements.
  3. Pick CSV (sometimes called Excel or spreadsheet format), or OFX/QFX (Quicken or Money) if that is all there is, and the longest date range offered. Twelve months catches yearly renewals; three months is the minimum for monthly ones.
  4. Drop the file above. Overlapping downloads are merged without double counting.

      How it decides

      Charges are grouped by merchant after stripping card numbers, store IDs and payment-processor prefixes. A group counts as recurring when it lands on a steady schedule (weekly to yearly) at a steady or slowly changing price. Shops you visit often are ruled out even when the price repeats.
